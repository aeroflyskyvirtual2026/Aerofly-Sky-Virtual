(HOMEPAGE)

WELCOME TO AFSC VIRTUAL AIRLINE

This is Aerofly Sky Virtual (ASV) a Virtual Airline under Aerofly FS Community (AFSC)

 Aerofly Sky Virtual is here so members can enjoy the experience of airline operations, conduct virtual flights, and connect with fellow aviation enthusiasts, Our goal is to provide a realistic and enjoyable environment for aspiring pilots to develop their skills, participate in events and build their virtual flying experience

ASV is a structured virtual airline operation, aspiring pilots makes a flight plan with SimBrief, and build a record through accepted flights, progression, awards and community
activity

Whether you are an experienced virtual pilot or just starting out, you are welcome to join, Please take a moment to review our rules and familiarize yourself with the operations



(GUIDELINES)

Aerofly Sky Virtual (ASV)

Official Guidelines & Regulations

Welcome to ASV, the virtual airline of AFSC, Our goal is to provide a realistic, organized, and enjoyable airline experience through flight simulation

By joining ASV, you agree to follow these rules

1. Membership & Conduct

Treat all members, ASV Owner, Staff with respect

No harassment, discrimination, threats, or unnecessary drama

Keep all airline related communication appropriate and professional

2. Flight Operations

Follow realistic flight procedures, including departure, cruise, descent, and arrival

Use realistic callsigns and flight numbers when provided

Do not intentionally crash

If you cannot complete a flight, inform ASV Owner or ASV Staffs when possible

3. Flight Reporting

Pilots must submit a complete flight  plan over our Official Aerofly Sky Virtual Simbrief Account

Staff may request additional information if a report appears incomplete or inaccurate
